Funding and referrals

Choose the way you're coming to us. Each guide covers what you need before your first visit, what it costs and how your sessions work.

Pathway guides

Private or health fund Paying yourself or claiming through private health. No referral needed. Medicare GP plan Rebated sessions through your GP's chronic condition management plan. DVA Veteran Gold and White Card holders, with no gap to pay. WorkCover Workplace injuries in Queensland, billed to the insurer. Car accident (CTP) Motor vehicle accident injuries under Queensland's CTP scheme. Sports injury insurance Club and association personal injury cover.

At a glance

The short version. Open a guide for the details.

Pathway Referral needed? Cost to you Bring
Private or health fund No Our fee, less your health fund rebate Health fund card
Medicare GP plan Yes. GP chronic condition management plan and referral Our fee, less the Medicare rebate (up to 5 sessions a year across all allied health) Referral and Medicare card
DVA Yes. Usually from your GP; lasts 12 sessions or 12 months Nothing. No gap Veteran Card and referral
WorkCover A lodged claim and your doctor's work capacity certificate Nothing for approved treatment on an accepted claim Claim number and certificate
Car accident (CTP) A lodged Notice of Accident Claim Form and insurer approval Nothing once the insurer approves treatment Claim number and insurer details
Sports injury insurance No referral to see us; your club's claim form Pay on the day, then claim back part of the gap Claim form and health fund card
