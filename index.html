Hips, knees and ankles are our focus

Most of our week is spent on the lower limb, so we see these problems often and know how they tend to respond. We look after the rest of the body too.

Hip

  • Hip and groin pain
  • Hip osteoarthritis
  • Gluteal tendon pain
  • Hamstring injuries
  • Rehab after hip arthroscopy or replacement

Knee

  • ACL injury and reconstruction rehab
  • Meniscus and ligament injuries
  • Kneecap (patellofemoral) pain
  • Patellar tendon pain
  • Knee osteoarthritis and replacement rehab

Ankle and foot

  • Ankle sprains and instability
  • Achilles tendon pain and rupture
  • Plantar heel pain
  • Fracture rehab
  • Shin pain and bone stress injuries

Also treating lower back, neck, shoulder and elbow pain, plus general strength and mobility. See all services and fees

What your first few weeks look like

No mystery about what happens next. Every step is explained before we do it.

Book your first assessment
  1. 1

    Book online or call

    Choose your pathway, a time and a practitioner. We'll send a short intake form so your session isn't spent on paperwork.

  2. 2

    A thorough first assessment

    45–60 minutes to understand what's going on, including strength and movement testing. You'll leave with a clear explanation, a plan and your first exercises.

  3. 3

    Rehab you can measure

    We re-test regularly so you can see what's changing. Your physio and exercise physiologist work from the same plan, so nothing gets lost between sessions.

  4. 4

    Back to what you want to do

    Return to sport, work or everyday life is guided by your test results and the goals you set with us, not just the calendar.
